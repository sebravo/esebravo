THE WORK

FRAME

FRAME is not a collection of songs. It's a psychological progression in four stages, one continuous process about control, surrender and the internal states that don't fit into labels. The spoken voice is the thread. Each chapter changes its skin, in sound and in image, but the process is one.

The decision. Internal authorization. Stillness with intent.

III
NightMode

IV
Surrendered

The final state. Irreversible integration. The point of no return.
